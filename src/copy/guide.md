# Solun Tone Guide

## The Voice

Solun's voice is **playful, intimate, witty, cute, premium, and writer-centric**. Think of a conversation with a trusted friend who understands both the craft of writing and the quirks of being a writer. We're approachable without being unprofessional, clever without being cloying, and intimate without being invasive.

### Core Principles

1. **Writer-Centric**: We speak to writers as writers. We understand their struggles, celebrate their craft, and respect their process.
2. **Premium but Accessible**: We're a premium product, but we're not snooty. Quality doesn't mean formality.
3. **Playful with Purpose**: Our playfulness serves clarity and delight, never confusion or annoyance.
4. **Intimate but Respectful**: We're close enough to say "Hey" but respectful enough to never overstep.

## Examples

### ✅ Do

**Greetings:**
- "Hey [name], welcome back"
- "Welcome back, writer"
- "Nice to see you again"

**CTAs:**
- "Start writing"
- "Begin your story"
- "Download for free"
- "Get in touch"

**Errors:**
- "This page wandered off somewhere"
- "Connection hiccup—check your internet"
- "Something's not quite right"

**Success:**
- "Nice!"
- "Perfect!"
- "All set!"

**Form Validation:**
- "Name is needed" (instead of "Name is required")
- "That doesn't look like an email"
- "Let's fix a few things"

**Empty States:**
- "Your vault awaits"
- "Check back soon—we're brewing something special"

### ❌ Don't

**Overly Formal:**
- ❌ "We regret to inform you"
- ❌ "An error has occurred"
- ❌ "Please ensure all fields are completed"

**Scary/Technical:**
- ❌ "Error 404: File not found"
- ❌ "System exception detected"
- ❌ "Invalid input detected"

**Filler:**
- ❌ "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet"
- ❌ "Placeholder text"
- ❌ "Sample content here"

**Crass/Edgy:**
- ❌ "This sucks"
- ❌ "You messed up"
- ❌ Any profanity or inappropriate language

**Overly Casual (to a fault):**
- ❌ "Yo, what's up bro?"
- ❌ "Dude, you forgot something"
- ❌ Excessive use of "babe/bro" in long paragraphs

## Inclusive Language

### Guidelines

1. **Avoid Assumptions**: Don't assume gender, writing genre, or skill level
2. **Use Neutral Terms**: Prefer "writer" over "author" or "novelist" (covers all)
3. **Accessible Wording**: Avoid jargon unless it's writer-specific and helpful
4. **Respect Pronouns**: If personalization is used, respect user preferences

### Examples

✅ "Welcome back, writer"  
✅ "Your stories, your vault, your control"  
✅ "Every writer's process is different"

❌ "Hey novelist!" (assumes they write novels)  
❌ "Welcome back, author!" (more formal, less inclusive)  
❌ "As a writer, you know..." (assumes experience level)

## Accessibility

### Writing for Screen Readers

1. **Be Concise**: Short, clear messages read better
2. **Use Descriptive Error Messages**: "Email is needed" not just "Error"
3. **Avoid Emoji-Only Messages**: Always include text alternatives
4. **Context Matters**: Error messages should be understandable without visual context

### Tone & Clarity

1. **Active Voice**: "Your words are saved" not "Words have been saved"
2. **Positive Framing**: "Check your internet" not "You're offline"
3. **Actionable**: Tell users what to do, not just what went wrong

## Usage Patterns

### When to Use "Hey" vs "Hello"

- **"Hey"**: Return visits, casual interactions, friendly features
- **"Hello"**: First-time users, formal contexts (legal pages), account creation

### When to Use Randomization

Some methods (`greet`, `cta`, `toast`) return random variants. This prevents repetition but maintains consistency. Use sparingly—only where variety adds value without confusing users.

### When to Add Detail

- **Toasts**: Short by default, add `detail` parameter only when context is crucial
- **Errors**: Always include actionable `detail` when possible
- **Forms**: Be specific about what's wrong and how to fix it

## Brand Voice Checklist

Before shipping any copy, ask:

- [ ] Does this sound like a friend helping a writer?
- [ ] Is it clear and actionable?
- [ ] Does it avoid corporate boilerplate?
- [ ] Is it inclusive and respectful?
- [ ] Would I want to read this if I were stressed about writing?
- [ ] Does it make the product feel premium without being pretentious?
